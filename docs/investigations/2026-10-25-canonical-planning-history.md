The PR517 record includes the exact approved tuple, caller/configuration digests,
selector fingerprints, immutable-source corrections, session restrictions and
historical limits. They are retained by immutable reference, not copied as new
instructions. No selector preimages, keys or private approvals are introduced.
The existing caller and its fixed checks are unchanged. Any later change to its
responsibility, capability or limits requires the applicable authority; this
index cannot approve it.

In particular, the historical PR517 approval is not reusable for a new session,
workflow rerun, repeated READY event, automatic ordinal advance, different
repository/PR/source tuple, changed selector or provider fallback. Unused or
incomplete historical work does not create a new allowance. Its admission
prerequisites and prohibitions are not removed by indexing the record; any new
execution requires its own applicable authorization. This index neither grants
that authorization nor activates the caller.

Past version plans are not a current release schedule. Adopted compatibility
and evidence semantics continue to be governed by the complete Canonical Set.
